# Story ##################################################################

HelpStory="In the near future, your job is to go to cities around the world and deliver medical supplies. 

At one delivery location in each city you will be given a special blue-top vial containing a drug component. Your mission is to collect 36 of these vials, so that the components can be combined to make a cure that will save humanity from a new virus.

You can walk or run around the city, or take a van or a bus. A public bus is cheap to ride, but you have to go where it goes. A private van costs more, but you can control its route.

In this future, the currency for everything is energy. You are paid for each delivery with a power-up in kJ (kilojoules). Everything you do uses up kJ, even standing still. You pay for a van ride or a bus ride in kJ, the further you go, the more you pay.

From level 2 you can choose your own neighbourhood, anywhere in the world, by building direct from OpenStreetMap."

# Tips: Play  ##################################################################

HelpTipsPlay="Collect batteries to gain more energy: you need 2000 kJ to access level 2, 3000 for level 3, etc.

Choose direction at next junction by colour.

Use [Advance] to build a route ahead: direct to delivery location, or via batteries to gain kJ.

Use speed controls to speed up, slow down or turn around while walking.

As you move around, watch out for virus-positive people: be careful in crowds and on buses.

If you don't want to wait, you can jump a red light, but if there is a camera, you will pay a fine.

Board a van at a designated zone: a blue icon and a van symbol on the road.

When riding in a van, use [Van Route] to change the van destination where you get out.

Board a bus at a bus stop, some are used by more than one bus route.

When waiting at a bus stop, you can choose which route you will take if there are 2 or more.

When riding in a bus, you can choose the stop where you will get off.

When riding in a van or a bus, you can press [Abandon] to get out, but you will pay a fine."
